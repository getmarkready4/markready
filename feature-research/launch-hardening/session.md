## Decisions already made — do not relitigate

- CSP is deliberately permissive on `script-src`/`style-src` (`'unsafe-inline'`,
  `'unsafe-eval'`). Next inlines both; a nonce CSP needs `proxy.ts` changes and
  risks hydration. The strict directives that matter (`frame-ancestors`,
  `base-uri`, `form-action`, `object-src`) are locked down. This is a conscious
  trade, not an oversight.
- Rate limiting is per-instance in-memory and is NOT an access control. Say so in
  the module comment, in those words.
- `/api/score` is deliberately NOT rate limited — `reserve_scoring` already
  enforces the allowance atomically in Postgres and returns 409 on a concurrent
  request. Adding a second limiter there would be redundant and could reject
  legitimate retries.
- The OG image is generated by `next/og`, not a checked-in PNG.
